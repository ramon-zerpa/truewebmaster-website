# Typography decision — approved for the visual direction

Approved Pencil direction: **Outfit 700** for headings and **DM Sans** for paragraphs, labels, navigation, and buttons. Outfit's bold geometric forms are closer in character to the existing “webmaster” logo lettering. The logo asset itself is unchanged.

The earlier directions used **Space Grotesk** and then **Manrope** for headings. Their comparison files remain in `previews/`; `previews/true-webmaster-home-desktop.png` shows the approved direction.

Future Astro work should load only the used font weights and test line wrapping in English and Spanish before finalizing the typography.
